# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- New **Place on Surface** tool: click to plant a single plant on any surface,
  or drag to paint several.
- Fixed: the Library panel stopped drawing below the thumbnail in Blender 5.x,
  because an icon that 5.x removed hid the Add and Scatter buttons.
- Fixed: spruce needles looked grey in EEVEE. Needle sprays are now flat-shaded
  with a rougher, less shiny material.
- New Scots pine bark: deep furrowed plates at the base and flaking copper bark
  higher up. Pine branches now match the upper trunk.

## 0.1.0

First release.

- 15 procedural species with 43 variants: grass, wildflowers, clover, fern,
  six trees and shrubs, stones and leaf litter.
- VD Scatter layers with these controls:
  - Density and minimum spacing.
  - Viewport amount.
  - Scale, rotation and tilt randomness.
  - Alignment to the surface normal and a slope limit.
  - A mask, which can be inverted.
  - Patchiness.
- Scatter your own objects on any mesh.
- Seven ready-made patches, square or round, with edge falloff.
- Scene-wide seasons: spring, summer, autumn and winter.
